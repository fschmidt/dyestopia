title: Passive: a match creates a tile one tier higher
ordinal: 2600
labels: [passives, engine]
---

Clearing a line leaves behind one or more tiles of the next tier up, where a recipe allows it - matching red yields an orange, matching orange yields an amber or a vermilion.

The most interesting idea on this list, because it is a direct answer to `D-002`. It is a flow rather than a stock, its rate is a parameter, and the supply is *earned* - the player causes it by matching, so the board is not steering and the telegraphing objection does not apply. It also happens to fix the tertiary problem specifically, since it is the only proposal that manufactures depth rather than breadth.

Open questions before it could be promoted: where the new tile lands, which is the whole placement question in disguise; whether it fires on cascade waves as well as on the move itself; and whether it should be a passive at all rather than a base rule, given `D-002`'s objection to modes that mask the base economy.
