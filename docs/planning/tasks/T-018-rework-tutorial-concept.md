## Description

The six lessons run — Match, Mix, Chain, Rainbow, Chain Breaker, Rainbow Chain
Breaker — but the concept behind them has not been settled, so the work below it
is not worth doing yet. Deferred until the shape of the tutorial is decided,
then re-cut into whatever cards that decision implies.

Consolidates the two cards this replaces:

- **Prepared boards** (was `T-001`, In Progress). Each lesson's board needs
  authoring and playtesting against its real goal, so the goal is reachable
  without luck and without teaching a bad habit.
- **Terminology and per-mechanic feedback** (was `T-002`, Todo). Each lesson
  carries a `term` — the one capitalised word it teaches. Highlight that term
  where it appears, and give each mechanic its own step and success copy instead
  of the shared generic lines. Canonical terms are in
  [the glossary](../../wiki/game/glossary.md).

Open question the rework has to answer first: whether teaching stays a set of
scripted lessons at all, or moves into the stage progression itself.

`T-011` (friends playtest) is blocked on this — sending people in without a
working tutorial wastes the one first impression each of them has.

## Acceptance criteria

<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 The tutorial concept is decided and written down
- [ ] #2 Every lesson's goal is reachable on its board from the opening deal
- [ ] #3 No lesson can be passed by an unrelated accident
- [ ] #4 The lesson's term is visually distinct wherever it appears, and success
      copy names the mechanic rather than saying "well done"
- [ ] #5 Played end to end by someone who has not seen the game
