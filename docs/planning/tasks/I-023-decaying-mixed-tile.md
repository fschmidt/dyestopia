title: Tile: mixed colour with a decay counter
ordinal: 2300
labels: [engine, balance]
---

A mixed tile carries a turn counter and reverts to one of its ingredients when it runs out. Turns result tiles from a permanent holding into a perishable one, so sitting on a good board costs something and the player is pushed to spend what they make.

Cuts against `D-002` as written: the non-seed pool already only shrinks, and decay is a second drain on top of the merge drain. It only makes sense paired with a supply mechanism, not instead of one - but paired, it is the natural brake, because a flow with no decay eventually floods the board. Worth holding as the counterweight to whichever mechanism `T-031` points at rather than as an idea in its own right.
