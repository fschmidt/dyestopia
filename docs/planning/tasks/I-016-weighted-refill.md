title: Weighted refill toward the dominant colour
ordinal: 1600
labels: [engine]
---

Mixing raises the chance that refilled tiles match whichever colour holds the most tiles on the board. The weighting compounds over consecutive mixes and resets when a move contains no mix. Would make chains self-reinforcing - needs care not to remove the tension that makes a Chain Breaker a decision.
