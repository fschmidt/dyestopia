# Glossary

The canonical terms. These are the words the UI uses, the tutorials teach, and
the rest of this wiki assumes.

**Board** — The stage's playable grid of cells.

**Tile** — One coloured piece occupying a cell.

**Cell** — One position on the board. A gap is not a cell.

**Primary** — A base colour that cannot be mixed.

**Secondary** — A colour mixed directly from two primaries.

**Tertiary** — A colour mixed from a primary and a secondary.

**Active colours** — Colours the current stage permits at all.

**Seed colours** — Colours a refill may drop.

**Match** — Three or more identical tiles in a horizontal or vertical line.

**Clear** — Removal of every tile in a match. Overlapping lines count each tile once.

**Legal move** — An adjacent swap or mix that immediately creates a match.

**Swap** — Two adjacent tiles exchange cells. Direction-blind.

**Mix** — Two adjacent colours become their recipe's result. The dragged tile dyes the target, so direction matters.

**Gravity** — Tiles fall into empty cells below them.

**Refill** — Empty cells receive new seed-colour tiles.

**Cascade** — A match created automatically by gravity or refill.

**Colour Chain** — The run of consecutive mixes since the last swap.

**Rainbow Chain** — A Colour Chain at the stage's maximum multiplier.

**Chain Breaker** — A swap that cashes in a live Colour Chain, at double the current multiplier.

**Rainbow Chain Breaker** — A Chain Breaker performed at maximum, at triple.

**Reshuffle** — A free rearrangement when no legal move remains.

**Tool** — A limited per-run ability, granted by a stage.

**Combo wave** *(prototype)* — A mixed colour absorbs connected tiles matching its ingredients. Off by default.
